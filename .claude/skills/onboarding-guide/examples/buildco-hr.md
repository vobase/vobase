## What the system does

A SELF-IMPROVING AI ASSISTANT
BuildCo HR AI doesn't just answer — it **learns**. Every time a staff member corrects it, leaves a note, or overrides a reply, the AI updates its own memory. The next conversation is a little better. Over weeks, it picks up the company voice, the recruiting playbook, who handles what, and the small exceptions that make BuildCo *BuildCo*.

There are three layers of memory: what it knows about itself, what it knows about each candidate or employee, and what it has learned from each staff member it works with.

ADAPTIVE SOFTWARE, NOT STATIC FORMS
Most HR tools are forms. This one shapes itself around how BuildCo actually works. Staff teach it by example — a private note, a correction, a one-line rule — and the workflow adjusts. New policy on dormitory check-ins? Tell the AI once. New escalation rule for site-safety questions? It remembers from then on.

ONE INBOX FOR WHATSAPP AND WEB
Every conversation — candidate on WhatsApp, employee on the portal, walk-in via email — lands in a single inbox. Staff reply once, in plain language, and the message goes back through whichever channel the person used. No tab juggling, no "which system was that in".

BUILT-IN HR FEATURES
- **Recruiting** — live pipeline across all open roles. Add candidates, schedule interviews, move stages with one click. The AI watches the pipeline and surfaces what's gone stale.
- **Onboarding** — checklist per new hire (paperwork, access, training, intros). Upload documents, mark items done, the AI fills in the gaps it can.
- **Leave** — staff request, manager approves, balances update instantly. No spreadsheets.
- **Internal notes** — `@`-mention a teammate inside any conversation. They get pinged on WhatsApp once they verify their number. Notes are private to staff; the candidate never sees them.
- **Policy knowledge** — the AI reads from a shared knowledge base (leave policy, work-pass rules, dormitory info, onboarding checklist, active projects). Update the file, the AI updates its answers.

GUARDRAILS
The AI knows what it shouldn't do on its own. Site-safety questions always escalate to a human. Work-pass questions route to HR Ops. Salary bands are never quoted. These rules live in plain text and can be edited without touching code.

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP CHANGES
When the AI wants to update a contact, schedule an interview, or change a record, it can either do it directly (for low-stakes actions) or **propose** the change for staff approval. Every proposal shows up in one place with a clean accept/reject view. Nothing happens behind your back.

AUTOMATIONS THAT RUN THEMSELVES
The AI checks in on its own schedule — a morning briefing at 8am, a sweep through stale conversations every 15 minutes, a nudge for interviews that didn't get scheduled. Staff don't have to remember; the AI does.
